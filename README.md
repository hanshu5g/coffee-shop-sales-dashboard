## 📌 Project Overview
An Excel-based sales analytics dashboard analyzing 149,116 
coffee shop transactions across 3 store locations in New York 
(Jan–Jun 2023) — uncovering revenue trends, product performance, 
peak hours, and store-level insights using Pivot Tables and 
an interactive Dashboard sheet.

## 💡 Key Business Insights
- Total Revenue generated: $698,812 across 6 months
- Coffee is #1 category contributing 38.6% of total revenue
- Ethiopia is the best-selling product ($42,304)
- Sales grew 103% from January ($81K) to June ($166K)
- Peak hours are 8 AM–10 AM — morning rush drives most sales
- Hell's Kitchen store leads with $236,511 in revenue
- Large size orders generate the highest revenue ($237,958)
- All 3 stores perform almost equally — no single dominant store

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- Total Revenue & Transaction KPI Cards
- Monthly Sales Trend (Jan to Jun 2023)
- Store-wise Revenue Comparison
- Product Category Breakdown
- Top Selling Products (Pivot Table)
- Peak Hour Sales Analysis
- Day-wise Sales Distribution
- Size-wise Revenue Chart
- Interactive Slicers — filter by Store, Month, Category
